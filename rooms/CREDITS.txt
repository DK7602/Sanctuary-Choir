Sanctuary Choir rooms - measured impulse responses
==================================================

Each venue's reverb is the measured response of a real room. The measurements
come from these datasets, used under their Creative Commons Attribution licences.
The licensors do not endorse Sanctuary Choir. The material is provided as is,
without warranties of any kind.

Grand Cathedral  (grand-cathedral.ogg)
  the church of Santi Marcellino e Pietro, Cremona
  From: ChurchIR: A Dataset of Multichannel Church Impulse Responses for Spatial Audio Applications, by Riccardo Giampiccolo, Sofia Parrinelli and Fabio Antonacci (2025)
  https://zenodo.org/records/14901751   DOI 10.5281/zenodo.14901751
  Licence: CC BY 4.0  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Used: source at the centre of the sanctuary, omnidirectional microphones 21 m down the nave, 5.4 m apart.
  Changes: direct sound removed; noise floor replaced by each 1/6-octave band's own decay; decay time x1.5; high-pass 40 Hz; unit energy; extended to 15.5 s; stored rising 9.589 dB/s (taken out on loading); Ogg Vorbis q8.
