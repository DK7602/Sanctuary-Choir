Sanctuary Voices - recorded choir voices for Sanctuary Choir
=============================================================

These recordings come from VocalSet, a singing voice dataset by
Julia Wilkins, Prem Seetharaman, Alison Wahl and Bryan Pardo (2018):

  VocalSet: A Singing Voice Dataset. Proceedings of the 19th International
  Society for Music Information Retrieval Conference (ISMIR), 2018.
  https://zenodo.org/records/1442513   DOI 10.5281/zenodo.1442513

VocalSet is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
licence (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes made for Sanctuary Choir: held notes were cut from the singers'
straight-tone long tones, scales and arpeggios, checked for steady pitch,
trimmed, sorted into soprano, alto, tenor and bass sections by voice and pitch,
and re-encoded as Ogg Vorbis. The pitch of each note is written in its file
name (m6312 = MIDI note 63.12). Alongside them:
  short/    the first moments of notes sung from silence (from the long
            tones, scales, arpeggios and fast scales), so a note starts the
            way a singer starts it;
  legato/   the slide from one note to the next, cut from the scales and
            arpeggios (to6500 = the note it arrives at, x100 = when the pitch
            is halfway there, in milliseconds);
  breathy/, belt/   the same from the breathy and belted scales and
            arpeggios.
Rebuild with tools/voicepack.
