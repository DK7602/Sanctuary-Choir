Sanctuary Voices - recorded choir voices for Sanctuary Choir
=============================================================

These recordings come from VocalSet, a singing voice dataset by
Julia Wilkins, Prem Seetharaman, Alison Wahl and Bryan Pardo (2018):

  VocalSet: A Singing Voice Dataset. Proceedings of the 19th International
  Society for Music Information Retrieval Conference (ISMIR), 2018.
  https://zenodo.org/records/1442513   DOI 10.5281/zenodo.1442513

Changes made for Sanctuary Choir: held notes were cut from the singers'
long tones, scales and arpeggios, checked for steady pitch, trimmed, sorted
into soprano, alto, tenor and bass sections by voice and pitch (the
countertenor sings with the altos), resampled to 32 kHz and re-encoded as Ogg
Vorbis. Up to five singers are kept per semitone. The pitch of each note is
written in its file name (m6312 = MIDI note 63.12). Alongside them:
  short/    the first moments of notes sung from silence (from the long
            tones, scales, arpeggios and fast scales and arpeggios), so a
            note starts the way a singer starts it;
  legato/   the slide from one note to the next, cut from the scales and
            arpeggios (to6500 = the note it arrives at, x100 = when the pitch
            is halfway there, in milliseconds);
  breathy/, belt/   the same from the breathy and belted scales and
            arpeggios;
  vibrato/  the same in the singers' own classical voice, with its vibrato,
            sung softly (slow piano scales and arpeggios, pianissimo long
            tones);
  fullvoice/  the classical voice in full voice (slow forte scales and
            arpeggios, forte long tones);
  syllables/  syllables of the songs the singers sang in straight tone
            (Dona nobis pacem, Caro mio ben, Row row row your boat), each cut
            from its consonants to the start of its vowel with the note and
            lyric times of Annotated-VocalSet (CC BY 4.0,
            https://zenodo.org/records/7061507); on<consonants> names them
            (onkxr = k then r).
Singers are named by VocalSet's anonymous ids (f1-f9, m1-m11).
Rebuild with tools/voicepack.
